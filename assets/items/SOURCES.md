# Evolution item sprites

These PNGs are bundled locally for offline shop and bag rendering.

- `fire-stone.png`, `water-stone.png`, `thunder-stone.png`, `leaf-stone.png`,
  `moon-stone.png`: https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/master/sprites/items
- `linking-cord.png`: https://www.serebii.net/itemdex/sprites/legends/linkingcord.png
  (reference: https://www.serebii.net/itemdex/linkingcord.shtml)

Pokémon artwork belongs to its respective rights holders; the files were
downloaded unchanged on 2026-09-20.
